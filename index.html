Who's playing?

Copy the rows from your Google Form responses sheet (header row included) and paste them here. The elves look for columns named like Name, Email, Wishlist / hints and (optionally) Avoid - people you shouldn't be paired with, e.g. your teammate who already knows your wishlist.

🔒 Names are drawn, so the roster is locked - nobody can quietly change an email address to intercept a letter.

The robot elf's mailbox

The app sends letters through a normal email account using SMTP.

💡 Use a dedicated mailbox (e.g. a fresh Gmail like blr.secret.santa@gmail.com) - not your own. Every letter also lands in the sender's Sent folder, so with "shred" turned on the elves delete those copies right after sending. For Gmail: turn on 2-Step Verification, then create an App Password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.

Draw the names

The draw makes one big gift loop through the whole office (A → B → C → … → A): nobody gets themselves, no two people swap gifts with each other, and "avoid" rules are respected. Pairings go straight into a sealed vault and into each Santa's inbox - this screen never shows them. Organisers can play too!
